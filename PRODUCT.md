# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

- **Gen Z / TikTok / Discord communities** — the core audience. Native to short-form, shareable, absurd content. French-speaking market.
- **Content creators & streamers** — looking for novel, reaction-worthy material for their channels.
- **Friend groups** — playing together, listening to calls, voting on outcomes, sharing clips.
- **RP / gaming communities** — drawn to character-driven, improvised scenarios.

Context: mobile-first, social media natives who value shareability and entertainment over utility. They discover the platform through TikTok clips, Discord servers, and streamer content.

## Product Purpose

EchoRoom is a social entertainment platform for AI-powered calls. Users create or discover absurd interactive scenarios, talk to AI characters with realistic voices, listen live with friends, and share clips virally. Success is measured by retention through community, UGC, and recurring engagement — not one-time prank gimmicks.

## Brand Personality

**Chaotic · Playful · Social**

Voice: irreverent, energetic, never corporate. "AI Social Chaos" is the tagline. The brand should feel like being at a weird party with your funniest friends — unexpected, shareable, and always entertaining.

Tone is French-native, youth-culture, internet-native. Short sentences. Bold claims. Zero corporate speak.

## Anti-references

- **prankcaller.fun** — too anonymous, one-time gimmick, no community, no retention
- **Traditional SaaS** — no sterile dashboards, no corporate gradients, no "enterprise" positioning
- **Deepfake / celebrity clones** — legal risk, bad vibes, against positioning. Hard boundary
- **Scam / harassment tools** — hard block. EchoRoom is a social game, not a prank weapon
- **The "serious AI" aesthetic** — no glass-heavy corporate AI landing pages, no "trusted by enterprises"

## Design Principles

1. **Social-first, not prank-first** — community, reactions, and sharing are the product engine, not the call itself. Every screen should surface social proof.
2. **Absurdity with guardrails** — maximum creative chaos inside a safe, moderated container. Fiction-first, consent-first.
3. **Content as retention** — UGC, trending feeds, featured scenarios, badges, seasons — every visit should surface something new and interesting.
4. **Gamified engagement** — leaderboards, streaks, seasonal events, creator economy loops. Participation should feel rewarding.
5. **Mobile-native by default** — TikTok/Discord interaction patterns first. Desktop is the power-user extension, not the primary canvas.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Dark mode is the default theme (already configured)
- WCAG AA target
- Skip link present on public surfaces
- Reduced motion support required for all animations
- Color blindness consideration needed for status indicators and badges
